Head, Ocular & Central Nervous System (SANS)

NOMINAL STATUS
Function & Spaceflight Risk Mechanism

Microgravity shifts ~2 liters of bodily fluid toward the head, increasing intracranial and intraocular pressure (Spaceflight-Associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome, SANS) and flattening the globe of the eye.

Non-Physician Measurement Guide

Apply water-soluble gel to the closed eyelid and use the high-frequency linear ultrasound probe to measure Optic Nerve Sheath Diameter (ONSD > 5.8 mm warrants flight-surgeon review).

Recommended Action & Escalation Protocol

Maintain lower-body negative pressure (LBNP) countermeasures and weekly ocular ultrasound checks. Escalate to ground flight surgeons if visual acuity drops or persistent headaches occur.
